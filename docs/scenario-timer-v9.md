# Timer scenario v9

This is an illustrative, assumption-based reweighting of the existing v8 scenario, not a statistically calibrated prediction. It does not claim that a report gives replacement dates or event probabilities. The original v8 probability, exposure data and v8 timestamp remain available unchanged. The v9 timestamp is **not** the date the displayed probability reaches 50%; the probability continues to come from v8. No new month, day or precision is attributed to a research paper.

OpenAI exposure, Anthropic observed Claude exposure, and BLS employment projections were already inputs to v8. Applying them again in v9 is a deliberate reweighting of the same three indicators, **not** new independent research or additional evidence. The Anthropic robotics index is newly added in v9; it is a different task-exposure dataset, not a forecast of job loss. Coefficients below are design assumptions, not measurements or estimates published in the reports.

Let `t0` be v8's baseline instant, `t8` its original scenario target, and `L` the sum of the available contributions below. The target is `t0 + (t8 - t0) * clamp(exp(L), 0.65, 1.35)`. Values outside the clamp do not increase its effect. No new indicator or default correction gives `L = 0` and therefore exactly the original v8 timestamp.

| Input | Log-time contribution | Scope and limitations |
|---|---|---|
| OpenAI direct exposure | `-0.6 * (exposure - 0.5)` | Approved, directly linked O*NET 31.0 metric only. A task exposure score, not adoption, job loss or an event probability. [GPTs are GPTs](https://openai.com/index/gpts-are-gpts/). |
| Korean-title proxy | `-0.12 * proxy_strength * (proxy_score - 0.5)` | A weak indirect title-similarity approximation based on the above source, not a source-measured score or validated international occupation crosswalk. If the approved source or exact proxy validation is missing it is not used. |
| Anthropic observed exposure | `-0.3 * (observed - 0.2)` when the score is positive; `0` when it is zero | US SOC observations of Claude task use, not all AI, adoption rate or job displacement. Zero may indicate observation below threshold, so it is recorded but does not shift the timer. `.xx` O*NET suboccupations receive a shared SOC parent score and are labeled `soc_group`. [Anthropic labor market impacts](https://www.anthropic.com/research/labor-market-impacts). |
| BLS projected total employment change | `0.12 * clamp(change_percent / 20, -1, 1)` | Context only: US SOC parent-level 2025–2035 total employment projections. They are not AI-caused change or a replacement probability. Missing change is not treated as zero. [BLS occupational projections](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm). |
| Anthropic robotics index | `-0.12 * (index - 1.5) / 3` when positive; `0` when the score is zero | 0–3 potential task-exposure index, not robot adoption, feasibility, employment loss or a probability. Zero does not distinguish nonphysical work from physical tasks without measured robot exposure; it is retained as an observation without shifting the timer. Only identical O*NET codes across source 29.3 and local 31.0 are used, and `same_code_cross_version` explicitly labels that the correspondence is not a validated version crosswalk. No KSCO application. [What work can robots do?](https://www.anthropic.com/research/what-work-can-robots-do). |

The historical v8 model also contains scenario-specific medical, government, actor and entertainment adjustments. V9 preserves them rather than claiming independent proof for them. To avoid two identical displayed countdowns, unchanged v8 dates are reserved first, then only adjusted dates may be shifted by the nearest available whole second in deterministic order. `timerAdjustmentSeconds` reports that display-only correction; it is not research evidence and is not part of `timerTimeFactor`. V9 outputs `originalTargetAt`, `timerModelId`, `timerModelVersion`, `timerTimeFactor`, `timerProbabilityAligned: false`, and `timerEvidence` containing actual input values, mappings, source URLs and assumption-derived log contributions. Input coefficients, report versions, selection criteria, and model limitations should be reviewed before any publication.
